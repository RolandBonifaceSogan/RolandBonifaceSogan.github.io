About Me

I am a PhD student in Statistics at Sorbonne University, working at the Laboratory of Probability, Statistics and Modeling (LPSM).

My doctoral research, titled "Statistical Inference in Graph Models: Graphon Estimation and Gaussian Graphical Models", focuses on statistical inference for graph models and complex networks. I am particularly interested in graphon estimation, stochastic block models, Gaussian graphical models, and high-dimensional statistical methods.

My supervisors are Tabea Rebafka and Fanny Villers.

Research Interests

  • Graphon Estimation
  • Gaussian Graphical Models
  • Stochastic Block Models
  • Network Analysis
  • High-Dimensional Statistics
  • Multiple Testing & FDR Control
